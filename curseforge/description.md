**Celestial Skill Tree** is a mod that transforms your progression into a map of the night sky. Each class is a constellation, each skill is a star, and the entire tree is generated based on your specific world—meaning no two worlds will ever have the same tree!

Spend your experience on skill points, plan a route across the stars, and build the character you want.

---

## Features

- **6 classes**: Duelist, Guardian, Arcanist, Marksman, Artificer and Berserker
- **A 7th class, the Mage**, when Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, Ars Nouveau or Malum is installed
- **Up to 100 skills per class**, each with a real effect
- **Keystones** that give a big advantage with a drawback
- **Ranked skills** with up to 5 ranks
- **A different tree in every world**, generated from the seed
- **12 birth signs** with a small permanent bonus
- **Falling stars, star showers and eclipses**
- **Constellated monsters**
- Fully data-driven, with KubeJS support
- And much more!

### - The Star Chart -

Press **O** to open the chart. Buy points with your XP, pick a starting star and grow outward: every skill has to
connect to one you already own. Nothing is applied until you press **Confirm**, so you can plan a whole route,
undo and redo before spending anything.

### - Strange Stars -

Not every star is a simple bonus. **Gates** link opposite sides of the map, **black holes** make the skills around
them stronger but seal the rest, and some keystones come in **exclusive pairs**: take one and the other is locked.

### - The Living Sky -

At night, **falling stars** cross the sky and leave a Star Fragment where they land, worth one skill point. Some
nights bring a **star shower**. Some days the moon covers the sun for an **eclipse**: monsters walk in daylight,
undead don't burn, but respecs are free until it ends.

### - Constellated Monsters -

Rarely, a monster spawns carrying a small constellation of its own. It is tougher than usual and drops a
Star Fragment when a player kills it.

### - Respec -

Unlearn a single skill and get back exactly what you paid for it, or reset everything with an
**Orb of Reconfiguration**.

## Getting Started

Press **O** (rebindable) to open the star chart. Press **H** inside it for the list of controls.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this mod in my modpack?**
A: Yes! Just keep the credit and don't reupload the jar.

**Q: Fabric / Forge / older versions?**
A: Not planned. NeoForge 1.21.1 only for now.

**Q: Can I change the skills?**
A: Yes. Classes, skills, keystones and rules are JSON files in a datapack. `/celestialskilltree validate` shows
exactly which file and field is wrong.

**Q: Is it multiplayer friendly?**
A: Yes. The server validates every purchase, and the server config controls prices, caps and every bonus.
